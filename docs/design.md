# Design direction

The product is a small appointment desk. The schedule should carry the page, with
connection health and booking actions close at hand.

- Ink `#192e42`, ocean `#145be0`, mist `#edf3fa`, paper `#ffffff`, teal `#14746d`, coral `#bd473e`.
- Manrope for the interface, with generous large numerals for dates. Fonts are served locally.
- A narrow blue navigation rail, a calendar sidebar, and a wide daily agenda. On small screens the date picker moves above the agenda.
- Left-aligned content, restrained borders, different shapes for navigation and appointment rows, and visible keyboard focus.
- A date rail makes the calendar itself the distinctive visual element. No decorative dashboard metrics, gradients, or marketing hero.

```
navigation | month picker | day heading + new appointment
           | calendars    | week date rail
           | connection   | chronological appointment rows
```

The first idea used a serif editorial heading. That felt unrelated to scheduling,
so the final direction uses one humanist sans family and gives the dates more weight.
